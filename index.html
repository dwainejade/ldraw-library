LDraw Parts Library mirror

A static copy of the official LDraw.org Parts Library, used by Brick Studio to convert LDraw models (.ldr, .mpd) in the browser.

Files are served at their library paths, for example parts/3001.dat and p/stud.dat. All names are lowercase.
